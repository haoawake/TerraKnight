# Changelog

> 1.0: playable from King's Pass all the way to the Radiance and Godhome: four classes, over a thousand Terraria items, enemy drops and a Bestiary assigned by Terraria progression, bosses with Terraria boss stats, town NPCs and shops, mining and building, pylon teleportation, four difficulties and Creative mode. The trailer is in [Releases](https://github.com/haoawake/TerraKnight/releases).
>
> 2.0: Terraria-style LAN multiplayer (direct IP connection, up to 16 players), "Terraria Settings" (Autofire, key rebinding), 50 save slots; landing after a wall slide recalculates the fall height from the slide.
>
> 2.0.1: the camera stays locked on the centre of the character; enemies from the neighbouring scene can walk across the door line; Stag Stations lead only to the other stations; every attack can ring bells, pull levers and smash jars; platforms hold you even when you drop onto them from a height, the grappling hook can latch onto platforms, and bosses do not get stuck on platforms; the Guide's "Crafting".

> 2.1.0: **save slots after slot 5 display and load normally after you quit to the main menu**; robust mod save writing (the full file is written before it replaces the old one, a .bak copy is kept, and when a save cannot be read the previous one is used automatically); Life Crystals lie scattered on the ground in early areas (in different places for each save), and every completed mask also gives one; a Stage unlocks when you defeat the final boss of the previous Stage; enemy respawn times are tiered by strength (30 seconds to 4 minutes), and a translucent ghost appears before an enemy respawns; stacks go up to 9999, and worthless items (Dirt Blocks, Stone Blocks, Wood) sell for nothing; Master mode has an extra accessory slot; the Goblin Tinkerer sells the Tinkerer's Workshop; the log records the cause of death of enemies that the player did not kill.

> 2.8.0: **Fishing**: the Angler sleeps by a lake in the Forgotten Crossroads; woken, he moves to Dirtmouth and asks for a quest fish a day, paying Terraria's rewards. Fishing power follows Terraria's formula (pole, bait, accessories, time and weather); each area stands for a Terraria biome with its fish, crates and junk, and crates open with a right click. Acid takes the Acid Hook (Terraria's Lavaproof Fishing Hook) or its tackle bag; enemies drop bait. **Every Terraria townsperson can move into Dirtmouth** (37, by stage: the Nurse heals, the Tax Collector collects, the Traveling Merchant visits by day every few days, Santa only in December, and the town pets), with a message in the lower left as they arrive or leave. **Hallownest's keys and keepsakes are Terraria items** (the Lumafly Lantern, City Crest, Simple Key, Elegant Key, Love Key, Shopkeeper's Key, Tram Pass, King's Brand, Delicate Flower, Rancid Egg, Pale Ore, the four relics, Kingsoul and Void Heart): **they count only while held**, and live only in the hotbar or the "Hallownest Bag" beside the Bestiary; whatever a save has already got goes into the bag on loading. **The Goblin Tinkerer reforges**: all of Terraria 1.4.5's prefixes (a weapon's damage, speed, crit, size, velocity, mana and knockback, a summon weapon's armor penetration and tag damage, an accessory's defense, mana, crit, damage, movement and melee speed), priced and coloured by Terraria's formulas and kept in the save; the Nail (size on the slash, speed on the wait between slashes) and the Dream Nail reforge too. **Quick Sell**: a button beside the Hallownest Bag turns on sell mode; a click on an inventory item sells it, and the sale row under the inventory buys it back for the same price. **Terraria 1.4.5's keys**: a Dash key (unbound by default as in Terraria; held, it dashes once, and held through the cooldown it dashes the moment it ends) with the Double Tap Dashing switch, Quick Buff (B), the map's zoom and transparency (numpad +/-, PageUp/PageDown), Next and Previous Loadout, Armor Set Ability; the keybinding list in Terraria's order, and pressing a binding's own key again unbinds it. **Master Mode boss relics**: in Master Mode every boss drops its relic each time it is beaten, all in new 2×3 pictures to set down; Godhome's Absolute Radiance, Enraged Guardian, Sisters of Battle and Winged Nosk have their own. Boss seals take the player to the arena's front door, and walking away keeps the seal; a right click on the open map switches the shallow and deep layers; the Nail swings with the inventory open; hammers shape blocks and platforms (half blocks, four slopes) and remove background walls when held, 292 walls (35 craftable), stairs into platforms, one-way platform walls under low ceilings; all of Terraria's craftable equipment can be made (631 items), their materials and sources added; placing shows a ghost; common materials drop from at least five enemies; the Shade is named "Shade".

> 2.7.0: **a boss fought in phases has a whole boss of its time's life in every phase**, each phase 20% more than the one before, the bodies up together sharing it (the False Knight's rounds of a broken armour and a beaten head, the armour the bulk and the head about three tenths of it; the Soul Master's and the Soul Tyrant's two phases; the Mantis Lords' eldest alone, then her two sisters together, three for the Sisters of Battle; the Watcher Knights one first, then two at a time; Oro alone, then with Mato); **bosses drop healing potions and hearts** (as in Terraria: 5 to 15 potions of their tier and 5 to 9 hearts, and a smaller share at the end of each phase); a heart heals 20 life when touched, the Heartreach potion pulls hearts from further away, and both are listed in the Bestiary and the drop record; **the rest of Terraria's armor**: the Meteor, Ninja, Mining and Prospector, Crimson, Snow and Pink Snow, Cactus, Pumpkin, Gladiator, Fossil, Angler and Captain, Ancient, wood and Ash Wood sets, the Old One's Army's tier 2 and 3 sets (sold by the Witch Doctor and the Cyborg), the Wizard Hat, the Magic Hat and the seven gem robes, and single pieces such as the Goggles, the Night Vision and Ultrabright Helmets, the Gi, Djinn's Curse, the Moon Lord Legs and the Dead Man's Sweater, with 1.4.5's set bonuses: the Meteor set's free Space Gun, the Crimson set's fast regeneration, the Cactus set's thorns, the Ash Wood set's lava protection, the Gladiator set's knockback immunity, the Old One's Army sets' stronger Ballistas, Flameburst towers, Explosive Traps and Lightning Auras, the gem robes giving any gem staff their gem's feature; robes are drawn as robes; **the rest of the crafting stations**: the Hellforge, the Adamantite and Titanium Forges, the Crystal Ball, the Bookcase, the Imbuing Station, the Autohammer, the Ancient Manipulator, the Solidifier, the Keg, the Cooking Pot, the Alchemy Table, the Heavy Work Bench and the themed stations; the Ammo Box, Bewitching Table, Sharpening Station, War Table, Slice of Cake and Crystal Ball give their buffs on a right click; **the Demon Altar and the Crimson Altar stand in the Temple of the Black Egg**; **information accessories show under the minimap**: the watches, the Depth Meter, the Compass, the GPS, the Weather Radio, the Sextant, the Fisherman's Pocket Guide, the Metal Detector, the Lifeform Analyzer, the DPS Meter, the Stopwatch, the Tally Counter and their combinations; the Magic Mirror, the Ice Mirror and the Cell Phone take the player home, the Rod of Discord and the Rod of Harmony teleport to the cursor; **40 boss summons**: usable anywhere once the boss is beaten, a portal takes the player into its native arena, through the arena's own entrance, and out of the portal where they land, straight into the fight with the story skipped, **and in a LAN game every player goes too** (only the user's seal is spent); **in a LAN game everyone plays in the host's world**: chests, crafting stations and furniture, items on the ground, Life Crystals and dug or built terrain follow the host, a chest opens for one player at a time, and a boss acts only on the players inside its arena while teammates outside stay where they are; **in multiplayer bosses have more life by the number of players** (Expert and Master, as in 1.4.5: ×1.35 for two, ×1.92 for three, ×2.63 for four, by the players there when the boss appears); **the map has a deep and a shallow layer**: the rooms behind doors (shops and homes, the Colosseum, the Grimm Troupe's tents, the Archives, the White Palace, the dreams, the trams) are the deep layer, the minimap draws the player's own layer, and the full map's tabs at the bottom left show the current, the shallow or the deep layer; the player's head is drawn on the middle of the body; **Terraria's liquid gear works in acid**: the Flipper, Neptune's Shell, the diving gear and the Flipper potion swim in acid unharmed, as soon as they are worn; the Water Walking Boots, Obsidian Water Walking Boots, Lava Waders, Terraspark Boots and the Water Walking potion stand on acid, and holding down sinks in; **enemies the base game's Nail never pushes take no Terraria knockback either** (turrets, Fool Eaters, egg sacs, Gulkas, heavy enemies such as Mantis Warriors and Husk Guards, and wall crawlers); **crafting works with a chest open**, its items counted; common enemies and elites drop twice the ore; teleporting mid-conversation by a pylon, a mirror or a boss seal closes the dialogue box; **boss defense is half its Terraria counterpart's**, so fast weapons like the Minishark and Ichor Arrows stay useful against late bosses; **a boss room locks only where its boss fights** (what the camera shows while the fight's lock holds), the rest of the room digs and builds as usual, and a boss in an ordinary room locks only its surroundings; **no hit invincibility frames**: every shotgun pellet and every minion's every attack deals damage; **mana as in 1.4.5**: magic weapons work without enough mana, which drops to 0 and makes that use 2.5 times slower, a mana potion restores the speed at once, channelled weapons slow down while out of mana, and the Mana Flower drinks for them; **the Bestiary is complete**: the Soul Tyrant, the Abyss's Siblings, the Spiny Husks rising from the corpse piles of Queen's Gardens and Deepnest, Godhome's Crystal and Enraged Guardians and the Absolute Radiance are recorded and drop loot, dream and summoned rematch kills count, a boss in several bodies (the Oblobbles, the Mantis Lords, the Watcher Knights) counts once per fight, and the Bestiary's life, defense and damage follow the current difficulty and balance; **boss drops that don't fit a full inventory after a dream or Godhome fight drop at the player's feet back in the Hall of Gods or the world**; craftable items come only from crafting, with their materials in the drop tables; the settings show the current version; only one Nail and one Dream Nail are kept.

> 2.6.1: **every loadout's crafting menu lists every recipe**: weapons, armor and accessories of any class can be crafted in any loadout, and a Creative mode save's crafting menu holds every item in every loadout.

> 2.6.0: **drops reworked by Terraria's stages**: every enemy's drop table adds what the enemies and chests of its area's Terraria biome give, of every stage, and the items of a stage not unlocked yet show in the Bestiary as a question mark with when they unlock; the early movement accessories (Hermes Boots, Flying Carpet, Dunerider Boots, Cloud in a Bottle…) drop from Stage 0; elites and bosses give one piece out of a "1 of N" for certain on every kill; **what Terraria makes only by crafting is crafted from the materials enemies drop, step by step up vanilla's recipes** (a Minishark into a Megashark, a Spell Tome into a Crystal Storm), with the materials those recipes need added (crimtane, Tissue Samples, Spell Tomes and more), and items that go into a recipe read "Material"; **Geo follows Terraria's coin ratios**, each area's total matching vanilla Hollow Knight's; **Quick Stack puts Geo into a nearby Piggy Bank or chest**; **stars fall at night in the rooms under the sky** and land as Fallen Stars. > 2.5.2: **the False Knight's head stays by him**: the head is a part of him and, like him, takes no knockback from Terraria weapons; every time he is stunned it comes out of his body, and there is only one head.

> 2.5.1: **in a Dreamer's dream the Dream Nail focuses** (once a Terraria weapon or the nail has struck the Dreamer down, hold the left or right button with the Dream Nail in hand to focus and drain it); **a steady frame rate in Deepnest's tram pass rooms** (the enemies the game keeps waiting outside the map, and the ground under them, stay as the game made them); **copied Husk Sentries patrol and chase as usual, with their usual sounds**; **the Work Bench, the anvils and the Tinkerer's Workshop can be stood on**, dropped through with down held, and carry other furniture such as an anvil or a Bottle; a workstation whose ground is dug out falls back into its item together with what stands on it; the cell under a chest can't be dug; blocks can't be placed inside a workstation or a chest.

> 2.5.0: **each boss has its own life, defense and damage by its size and fighting style** (agile bosses have less life and defense and hit harder; big, heavily armoured ones have more life and defense); the bosses the base game staggers are staggered again, paced by damage taken, the others have super armour the whole time, and multi-phase bosses share their life out over their phases; **the Ram Rune's slam breaks the floors vanilla breaks with Desolate Dive**, getting Desolate Dive gives the Ram Rune, and a pickaxe digs these floors open too; minions and sentries do not knock enemies back; **an area's map bought from Cornifer shows unexplored cells at 70% transparency, becoming fully opaque after exploration** (Life Crystals still have to be found); Shift+left-click also sells while a shop is open; **crafting also uses materials in chests within 8 tiles of you** (the inventory's first, then the chests'); items on the ground are drawn in front of the scene's plants, walls and ground; Life Crystals lie only in the Stage 0 and 1 areas, and the unmined ones an older save placed in later areas move there; the Bestiary shows a boss's whole life and updates an enemy's numbers each time you meet it; enemies sent out again and again from a pool, such as the Aspid Mother's hatchlings, count in the Bestiary and drop loot for every kill; a Whispering Root's essence orbs stay hidden until the Dream Nail strikes the root.

> 2.4.1: **the Nail deals Terraria damage by stage** (each of the Nailsmith's four upgrades adds 25%; hits show damage numbers and can crit; the recoil matches the vanilla nail's); the Awoken Dream Nail deals 50% more; the Nail and the Dream Nail are not in the crafting menu; a long list of drops in the Bestiary scrolls with the mouse wheel; **24 Life Crystals lie in the world**, and how many can be eaten grows with masks and bosses beaten (up to 15); the launcher detects the Modding API's conflicting files and explains them.

> 2.4.0: **Terraria-style lighting** (light from torches, the Mining Helmet, bullets and spells softly lights up the surroundings tile by tile, and spots in dug tunnels and dark areas that no light reaches are dark); **bosses can be fought only from inside the battle area** (attacks from outside the gate do nothing, and minions cannot hit them either); **a sleeping boss loses only 1 HP when woken**, and takes no damage during its wake-up animation; after the Gruz Mother fight the battle gates on both sides open normally, and False Knight's head retracts as usual after it has been exposed for 5 seconds; **vanilla scene transitions and camera** (the seamless world remains as an experimental feature, off by default); **boss rooms keep their vanilla terrain** (bosses behave at platform edges and walls exactly as in vanilla); after you load a save again from the main menu the controls work as usual, and each preparation step of starting a new Creative mode save is protected separately; money in the Piggy Bank pays vanilla fees such as benches and Stag Stations; a new permanent "Nail" item (the vanilla nail; it can parry Revek); the three dream bosses are at mid-game strength; potion sickness from healing potions, Isma's Tear in acid, conveyor belts, falls in dream parkour, the three Mantis Lords' health bars, selling (no accidental buyback), minions pulling levers, the number of hits to mine wood blocks, the knockback of several enemies, WASD on the map and Smart Cursor block placement all work as intended; the vanilla drop at the doorway when you enter a room does not count toward fall damage; terrain is identified by the room it is in (boss rooms and Dirtmouth's townsfolk all recognise their own rooms), and you always get up from the bench after loading a save; key rebinding accepts the middle mouse button and the side buttons; pressing Jump while the grappling hook is pulling you jumps off it; dying before you have sat on a bench returns you to where the game started (a bench becomes your respawn point only once you have sat on it); the ghost shown before a respawn has no collision, and when a spawn point is blocked by blocks or platforms the enemy respawns nearby; when Terraria weapons hit hard things such as spikes and armour, the character stays in place; Terraria weapon effects (projectiles, swings, whips, Zenith, particles) are drawn on the top layer, always in front of vanilla's foreground vines and silhouettes.

> 2.3.0: **the Dream Nail** is a permanent item in your inventory; holding it, left / right click is the vanilla Dream Nail (reading minds, entering dreams, Dreamgate), and its damage rises with the Stage; **False Knight's death sequence is complete** (it drops its loot, is recorded in the Bestiary, and its corpse has no health bar; it has super armour during its rage, and after it is knocked down the sequence continues as usual); if minions or projectiles kill a boss during its opening roar, the character regains control as usual; Hornet in Kingdom's Edge is recorded in the Bestiary and drops loot as usual when beaten; **Stages follow the abilities the Knight gains** (Mothwing Cloak, Mantis Claw, Crystal Heart, Monarch Wings and Shade Cloak each open a Stage, and areas are assigned to Stages along this route); **Geo is a physical item** (up to 2.1 billion per stack; it fits in chests and in the new Piggy Bank); **boss rooms cannot be dug, built in or blown up**; the shared enemy invincibility window introduced then has since been removed, allowing independent shotgun and minion impacts; ores (enemies drop ore, smelted into bars at a Furnace) and five armour sets: Tin / Lead / Silver / Tungsten / Platinum; the "Misc Equipment" page (a hook slot, with mounts and pets to come); chests have "Restock" and "Sort Items", and the inventory has "Quick Stack to Nearby Chests" and "Sort Items"; item prices are rebalanced; drops differ less between difficulties (a bit more money drops); Life Crystals show on the map; items crafted only from worthless materials also sell for nothing; WASD works as usual after you change Hollow Knight's own keyboard settings; dropped items on the ground are cleared when you quit the game.
