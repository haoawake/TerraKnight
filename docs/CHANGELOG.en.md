# Changelog

## 5.3.0 — Hollow Knight's map, the Wardrobe and faster pylons

### New

- **Hollow Knight's map**: M opens Hollow Knight's own map, with the pylons, townsfolk, grubs, benches, Grimm flames, the Wayward Compass's way and teammates marked on it as before; the minimap and the overlay map use it too, and in creative mode the whole map is open. Terraria Settings' "Full map" switches back to Terraria's map.
- **Wardrobe**: right-click any area's dresser to open the Wardrobe, holding every vanity piece the save has had, paged by set and area; left click takes a piece out and right click puts it on. Pieces taken out are copies with no value, free to mix. Duplicates are sold for their price as they come in. Shift + right click opens the dresser's old drawer.
- A shield that has blocked its bearer's whole life in damage breaks for 6 seconds, and hits land from every side meanwhile.
- The Guide's Advice switches the crafting column to the recipe book from the Piggy Bank too.

### Improvements

- On Terraria's map every room stretches a little to fit its real ground, so most neighbouring gates meet; a passage is drawn between the gates that still lie apart.
- A pylon's teleport takes about 1.3 seconds from the click to moving again, and the view opens beside the pylon.

### Fixes

- A Spiny Husk bursts its spines once after dying, as in Hollow Knight, and they hurt only as they burst; after the burst its corpse can be broken, without counting as a kill.

## 5.2.1 — Guide's Advice, movable windows and minion balance

### New

- The Guide sells the Guide's Advice (10000 Geo): carried, it puts a scroll beside the crafting column's hammer that switches the column to the recipe book; put a material in to list every recipe that uses it.

### Improvements

- The inventory's windows can be moved: drag one by an empty part (its title, its frame, the gaps between slots) and it stays where it is put; a double click on an empty part puts it back. Home Everywhere's storage opens right of the crafting column.
- The left column of Terraria Settings scrolls with the mouse wheel, so the multiplayer options stay on the screen.
- The Amethyst Stat Viewer, the Drop Blacklist and the Bestiary can have keys (unbound by default, set in Terraria Settings' key list); pressed with the inventory shut, the inventory opens with the window.

### Balance

- The Imp Staff, Hornet Staff, Mushroom Staff and Shade Soul Staff (stage 2) and the Optic Staff and Pirate Staff (stage 4) deal more damage: used alone, they match the other classes' weapons of their stage.
- The Zenith's move that circles you before flying out passes through its target twice, and from afar hits as hard as its other moves.

### Fixes

- In rooms full of their own sounds (Fog Canyon), weapon and spell sounds play as usual.
- The "Path of Pain" achievement unlocks on completing the Path of Pain.
- The new-AI Vengefly King keeps after the player once they leave its alert range, flies round rock in the way, and its swoop stops above the ground.

## 5.2.0 — Remade weapons, damage balance and Home Everywhere

### New

- **Remade weapons**: a new world is made with the remade weapons or Terraria's. With the remade ones every class's weapons have new techniques, effects and right-click arts, and minions hunt smarter; Terraria's are used as in Terraria, and older saves keep them.
- **Weapon refit**: the Goblin Tinkerer turns a single weapon between its Terraria and its remade version, keeping its prefix and forging.
- **Home Everywhere storage**: an area's chests and banks make one storage to search, sort and deposit into; right-clicking a home opens it.
- **Terraforming gun**: sold by the Steampunker from stage 4; it turns Terraria blocks and furniture into drops and restores the ground digging changed.
- The Collector's Map marks grubs and benches on the world map; Terraria Settings has Terraria's Forward Zoom switch.

### Balance

- **Weapon damage**: every weapon's damage was set from measurements; within a stage, rangers, mages and summoners deal about 20% more a second than warriors, who have the higher defense.
- Under the new enemy AI one wind-up looses one volley, and attacks come slower.
- The Lifeblood Core keeps growing a shield: a layer every 5 seconds, up to 3.
- Relics sell for five times the game's price, matching what Hallownest's shops ask.

### Improvements

- The Amethyst Stat Viewer shows the mana regained a second moving and standing still, what the Arcane Crystal adds and the permanent boosts already used.
- An item the Shimmer transmutes says what it becomes in its tooltip, marked "(unlocks at stage 5)" when it waits for that.
- The music keeps its volume through gunfire and explosions.
- The Aglet drops from small enemies from stage 1.
- The Vengeful Spirit's, Howling Wraiths', Shade Soul's and Abyss Shriek's weapons can be reforged at the Goblin Tinkerer.
- The Wayward Compass leads by the ways the save has open, and passes over Zote's fights once he is dead.

### Fixes

- Pylons can be set down in Godhome, dreams and the White Palace; teleporting to a pylon enters the room as Hollow Knight does, and coming back to a pylon by the Watcher Knights loads the fight as usual.
- The Black Egg Temple's door shuts once you have walked all the way in.
- A boss room's dive floors break as usual once its boss is beaten.
- The Nightmare King's arena has a ceiling: you can't fly out of the view.
- A magic weapon that fires several times in one use (the Arc Surge) pays its mana once; the Flamethrower, Elf Melter, Flower of Fire, Flamelash and Arc Surge can't be used in water, acid or shimmer.
- The Shadowbeam Staff's beam bounces straight off walls; the Lightning Strike's bolts fly at an enemy's middle; the Nail Gun's delayed blasts hurt the enemy the nails are in; the Stynger's shrapnel looks as in Terraria.
- The Rod of Discord and the Rod of Harmony teleport through their key as often as in the hand, the Rod of Discord giving Chaos State as usual.
- Minions and sentries fight small enemies as soon as they are in the room, and a boss once you have struck it.
- The cutscene poses the game loops (charging the Dreamgate, taking a mask shard and the like) play once and hold their last pose.
- A Spiny Husk's corpse doesn't burst into spines.
- New enemy AI: enemies come into sight as the game has them; Xero keeps attacking down to his last health; Elder Hu's rings slam down over a player flying high; an enemy sharing its animations (the Hive's Hivelings) uses only its own.
- In multiplayer, a warrior's dream beaten in a teammate's game counts as beaten in yours too, and what a boss flings or leaves on the ground hurts every player.

## 5.1.0 — Vanity, boss pets and boss fight fixes

### New

- **Terraria's vanity**: Terraria's 427 vanity items drop from Hallownest's enemies, big and small. Each costume belongs to one kind of enemy and drops as a whole set; boss masks drop from their bosses (1 in 7); hardmode-stage bosses can drop a whole developer set. Vanity appears at the stage of the enemy that drops it and stays out of chests and fishing crates. Worn vanity is drawn by Terraria's rules: whether a hat shows the hair, robes, capes, belts, long coat tails, glowing parts, beards and the male and female bodies all match the original. The bag's filter has a "Vanity" tab, and vanity items say "Vanity item" in their tooltip.
- **Regional costumes**: each of the 24 areas has its own costume (head, shirt, pants), sewn at a Loom from that area's block: 15 for the head, 25 for the shirt, 20 for the pants. Each is a Terraria vanity set in the area's colours, its capes, coat tails and women's pants included, and wears exactly like that set.
- **Boss pets**: each of the 48 boss fights (the dream and Godhome variants included) has its own pet, a pixel pet in the manner of Terraria's Master Mode boss pets, which that boss drops at a 1% chance. Walkers run and jump behind you and fly over when left behind; flyers fly beside you; some float by your shoulder and now and then make a little gesture. The Radiance's and the Absolute Radiance's pets are light pets.

### Balance

- A boss can be struck only from inside its arena, and minions attack a boss only after your own blow has landed on it. A boss that hasn't come on stage yet (such as the False Knight waiting in the roof) can't be hit.
- The Ancestral Mound's enemies come back and come in pairs like everywhere else, and more with the Water Candle.
- Enemies that appear together stand two or three bodies apart, flyers also above and below each other; in a crowded room every enemy gets its share of copies.
- Godhome's accessory lock bans wings only: dash accessories and multi-jumps such as the Cloud in a Bottle still work.

### Improvements

- The Super Dash has a key of its own (in the keys of "Terraria Settings"), the Dash key at first: a long press of Dash is the Super Dash, a tap a dash. Bound to another key, the Dash key dashes the moment it goes down, and the Super Dash key held charges, let go it flies, pressed again it stops.
- The Campfire, Heart Lantern, Star in a Bottle, Bast Statue, Sunflower and Garden Gnome give their buffs from the Piggy Bank too.
- Estee is drawn as in Terraria, with the star turning under her face and a starry trail.
- With the cursor over the hearts or the mana stars, the current life or mana and its maximum show beside it.
- The frame rate cap in the game's settings takes effect.
- The Descending Dark rune and the shields that dive: press Down twice in the air to dive; a single press is left to hovering and falling faster. The Descending Dark rune's dark dive has a 2-second cooldown.
- With the Nail arts learned, the Nail swings the moment attack is pressed and charges while held; a full charge let go with Up or Down held, or pressed just before, is the Cyclone Slash, with Left or Right held as well.

### Fixes

- Fixed Soul Twisters remaining invisible in Sleep while casting with the new AI. Their native wake target is retained, and extra attacks wait until the body is visible.

- Each save's world is its own: starting a new save or loading another never brings over the blocks, walls, liquids or torches placed in the last world.
- Life at zero always ends in death, even when a fall or the like lands during a moment of invulnerability; the player is never left standing at 0 life without regenerating.
- With the new AI, Hornet goes on through her dashes, jumps, thread storms, counters and needle throws after a counter or a thread storm, to the end of the fight at Kingdom's Edge too.
- An enemy struck from afar wakes and fights (with both the original and the new AI), including those that wake by their sensing range, such as the Hive Guardian.
- The Husk Guard faces the player when it charges and slams, and chases without running backwards or standing stuck; the Aluba faces the right way.
- Godhome's False Knight lands in the arena instead of under the roof.
- The Massive Moss Charger leaps back into the arena from the platforms at its sides.
- The Vengefly King's dives stay above Godhome's floor.
- The God Tamer fights in the arena the player is in.
- A boss caught in rock gets itself out.
- Godhome's Hornet (the Kingdom's Edge fight) and the Winged Nosk drop their own loot.
- Backspace deletes text in character creation, save and chest renaming and the bestiary search, with an input method too.
- With the Featherfall potion, holding Down to fall faster never causes fall damage.
- With Steady Body worn, the speed after a dash (the shadow dash too) falls back to the run's.
- Any dash out through a room's exit (the shadow dash and the Dashmaster's aimed dashes included) leaves gravity as it was in the next room: no floating.
- In a multiplayer boss fight every player's blows deal damage, also while one of the players is down or held.
- In a multiplayer game the other players in the room hear the bosses' and enemies' attacks and the boss music too.
- In a multiplayer boss fight a living boss shows no corpse and gives no death cries in the other players' games.
- In a multiplayer Mantis Lords fight every player sees the lords move the same way, with the new AI too.
- In Godhome's Hall of Gods the statues' and the Pantheon doors' challenge menus take mouse clicks on "Challenge" and the bindings, and close with U, a right click or Esc.
- Caught on a seam in the ground while walking or landing, the Knight steps over it and walks on.
- A full Nail charge let go during a dash does the other arts when the Dash Slash isn't learned.
- However many holes the ground has, the frame rate stays as with none: blasting the ground again and again with dynamite and bombs stays smooth.
- Every character of Hallownest's lines rewritten for the Terrarian (tablets, diaries and talk) shows in the game's font, none as an empty box.

### Journal and damage fixes

- **Actual loot-stage selection**: choose current or an earlier unlocked stage in the journal. Drop tables change while character and enemy stats, abilities, shops and boss records stay unchanged. Saved per world; Current follows progression. In multiplayer the host controls and synchronizes the choice.

- Animated hunter journal with native Terraria frames, available-now and all-records modes, exact progression requirements and boss-aware drop cache refresh.
- Separate exploration and boss progress, with localized notifications in the lower left.
- Early gem bolts hit each enemy once; combined twin, bounce and repeated-volley damage discounts multiply correctly.
- Cattiva and Trusty Cattiva use their forward punch hitbox only while punching. Idle body contact does no damage; the native nine-tick per-enemy cooldown is preserved.

Weapon combos, right-click arts and casting animations are deferred to a later release.

## 5.0.0 — The new enemy AI, the Charm Bag and achievements

### New

- **Starting a game is Terraria's**: Hollow Knight's title screen stays; "Start Game" leads to Terraria's character list, character creation and world list. Character creation changes the hair, the clothes and every colour (the sliders drag with the mouse), and the player is drawn in game as created; a new world takes one of five difficulties (Normal, Hard, Expert, Master, Hollow) and an enemy AI (Vanilla / New). The first entry of the difficulty row is Creative Mode.
- **The new enemy AI** (choose "New" when creating a world):
  - Every common enemy and elite has new attacks and a new way of hunting, made for a Terraria player's mobility: lunging, chasing, jumping, diving, stalking, blinking, burrowing and dropping from the ceiling, and leading their attacks to where you will land. New moves use only the enemy's own projectiles and animations; an enemy without projectiles uses body moves such as rams and leaping pounces. Only so many enemies attack at once, and a hit enemy pauses for a moment.
  - Higher difficulties are fiercer: from Expert, new moves cool down faster, animations play faster and enemies attack from farther; Master lets one more enemy attack at once; from Master, enemies break grappling hooks, chase the end of a dash and knock the player off a mount.
  - Enemies can inflict Terraria's debuffs: Slow, Chilled, Webbed, Confused, On Fire!, Poisoned, Electrified, Bleeding, Broken Armor, Darkness and Silenced.
  - The 38 bosses (dream and Godhome versions included) each get a stronger fight: their own moves and phases stay, the time between moves shrinks with difficulty (×0.9 on Normal to ×0.66 on Hollow), their own animations play faster (×1.1 on Normal to ×1.3 on Hollow), and new moves join at two thirds and one third of their life, Expert and above starting with the second set. New moves use only the boss's own projectiles, objects and animations (the Hollow Knight's advancing pillars of ground, Nosk's flung slime, the Radiance's orbs); in boss fights the mod draws no boxes, lines or glows of its own.
  - Bosses reach the whole room: a player on a high platform, hanging under the ceiling, clinging to a wall or flying with wings is attacked with the boss's own moves, anti-air moves coming into play after a second and a half off the ground. Bosses and their attacks pass through the blocks and platforms players place in a boss room.
- **Charm Bag**: with the inventory open, the charm icon left of the Housing button opens the Charm Bag: Hallownest's 40 charms and their notches. Charms are put on and taken off only while resting on a bench, as in the game; past the notches left a charm overcharms, which the first time takes three tries (two cracks, then it holds) and doubles the damage taken while it lasts (shown in the buff bar). The Kingsoul (5 notches; as the Void Heart 0, and it can't be taken off) and the Grimmchild (2) are worn there too; the White Fragment can't be worn. Quick Focus and Quick Slash cost 2 notches, Steady Body 4 (a set that doesn't fit on loading comes off with a message). A worn charm with a skill uses it with the Charm Skill key (C by default, rebindable on the keys page of Terraria Settings); one skill works at a time: hold the Favourite key (Left Alt by default) and click a worn charm in the bag to choose it. Each skill's cooldown shows in the buff bar, and the key pressed while it cools shows the seconds left over the player. The damage charms deal follows the current stage's base nail damage. A Creative save gets every charm, 11 notches, the three fragile charms unbreakable and overcharming open on loading. The setting [Gameplay] CharmBag switches the bag off.
  - Gathering Swarm (1 notch): skill: a glow spreads from the player over the whole room and picks up every dropped item it reaches (what the bag has room for), 5 s cooldown; the game's own Geo-carrying flies stay.
  - Wayward Compass (1): the map points out the nearest next boss not yet beaten, and the minimap and the full map draw the way there in gold dots.
  - Grubsong (1): three glowing butterflies circle the player; with life not full it heals 10% of max life, 10 s cooldown.
  - Stalwart Shell (2): 0.2 s more immunity after a hit; skill: a 3 s shell takes every hit in that time, and pressed again sends a wave at the cursor that deals the total taken to every enemy in its blast (one not sent waits 10 s), 20 s cooldown.
  - Baldur Shell (2): skill (held): a shield raised toward the cursor for up to 0.8 s, moving at 60%, hits from the front taking 70% less; in its first 0.15 s a hit is a perfect guard, blocked whole, and a projectile it blocks flies back for twice its damage; 5 s cooldown (3 s after a perfect guard).
  - Fury of the Fallen (2): at 35% life or less every class deals 25% more; skill: gives up 30% of max life (leaving at least 1), bursts a ring of fire round the player and doubles the damage dealt for 2 s, 10 s cooldown.
