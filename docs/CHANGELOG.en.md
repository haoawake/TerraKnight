# Changelog

## 5.0.0 — The new enemy AI, the Charm Bag and achievements

- **Charm Bag**: with the inventory open, the charm icon left of the Housing button opens the Charm Bag: Hallownest's 40 charms and their notches. Charms are put on and taken off only while resting on a bench, as in the game; a charm past the notches left overcharms, which the first time takes three tries (two cracks, then the break) and doubles the damage taken while it holds (an overcharmed debuff shows it). The Kingsoul (5 notches; as the Void Heart 0, and it can't be taken off) and the Grimmchild (2) are worn there too; the White Fragment can't be worn. Quick Focus and Quick Slash cost 2 notches, Steady Body 4 (a set that no longer fits on loading comes off with a word). A worn charm with a skill uses it with the Charm Skill key (C by default, rebindable on the keys page of Terraria Settings); one skill works at a time: hold the Favourite key (Left Alt by default) and click a worn charm in the bag to choose it. Each skill's cooldown shows as a debuff in the buff bar, and the key pressed while it cools shows the seconds left over the player; pressed while the game holds the player (a hit, a Nail Art) it is kept for 0.4 s. The damage charms deal follows the current stage's base nail damage. A creative save gets every charm, 11 notches, the three fragile charms unbreakable and overcharming open on loading. The setting [Gameplay] CharmBag switches the bag off.

  - Wayward Compass (1): the map points out the nearest next boss not yet beaten, and the minimap draws the way there in flowing gold dots.

  - Fury of the Fallen (2): at 35% life or less every class deals 25% more; skill: gives up 30% of max life (leaving at least 1), bursts a ring of fire round the player and doubles the damage dealt for 2 s, 10 s cooldown.

Arc Surge's bolt and hand flash last 48 ticks without mana (16 normally), retaining first-tick-only damage. Last Prism keeps normal convergence and uses 12 ticks of shared hit immunity without mana (5 normally).

- Fixed control loss after entering flooded rooms, walking in midair and snapping to water above solid blocks. Poured liquids now use cell-based physical buoyancy, retain control and gravity, allow diving, and respect solid collisions. Native pools retain their original swimming behavior.
- The Angler now uses 36 Hallownest quest fish with dedicated hints and updated dialogue. Native character dialogue and inscriptions address the player as a traveller from Terraria.
- Fixed shared multiplayer items failing to shimmer: the host resolves transformations and decrafting, including requests from scenes simulated by a guest. Remote boss controllers cannot move nonparticipants before scene ownership is registered. Multiplayer protocol is now net14; all players must use the same plugin version.
- Personal multiplayer treasure bags follow the synchronized boss body instead of a guest's unsynchronized local corpse, preventing rewards from falling below the scene. Death timing and positioning in the game running the fight retain their existing behavior.
- Audited loot stages and accessory crafting: aligned information accessories with the travelling merchant, added the Ice Mirror to ice-themed regional chests, gated early Fishing Chest bait, raised diluted Black Lens/Bone Feather sources to at least 1%, and restored 45 supported native recipes. Cross-pillar fragment conversion remains excluded; unresolved decorative sources are listed in the audit report.
- Weapon and armor forging caps for stages 0–5 are now +2/+3/+4/+5/+6/+6. Each weapon level still adds 5% base damage, for at most 30% on newly forged weapons. Armor keeps +1 defense per level (+2 for warrior armor). The Nail and Dream Nail follow the player's stage. Existing over-cap levels are preserved and cannot be raised further.
- Fixed the empty stage-0 Golden Crate equipment pool. Area coffers exclude duplicate equipment; uncollected items have twice the draw weight. Collection history is saved per character and recognized by the Angler, including combined accessories. Daily quest credits can accumulate to three, with legacy-save migration.
- Vengefly King and Soul Warrior now use complete boss reward rules, with one payout after all bodies fall. Godhome's stage 4-to-5 health growth is provisionally 1.8x; world health anchors and base defense remain. Moon Lord equipment and Luminite now come from terminal sources, closing old crate and ordinary-enemy bypasses while retaining owned items.
- Part of each pantheon's material/equipment bonus moves to native completion rewards, repeatable once per successful run. Overflow and completion rewards are saved; legacy bags retain their old material bonus. P5's health multiplier is unchanged.
- Lifeforce now boosts cyan life from Mask Shards, including partial masks, by 20%; the base-life cap applies before the potion bonus. Quick Buff compares inventory and Potion Box together, chooses the best food and longest luck duration, and consumes only one flask. Existing effects and a full buff bar no longer waste potions. Drinking is blocked when dead or when a restoration-only item has nothing to restore. Gills, Warmth and Biome Sight describe this world's applicability limits and are not consumed without a corresponding effect.
- Player progression now counts the five distinct native movement abilities in any order, once each. Obtaining Mantis Claw first advances to stage 1. Bestiary, shop locks and Guide hints describe ability counts; starter accessories and temporary movement effects do not advance progression.
- Bestiary search and Guide drop-source indexing now work across frames and show progress. Focusing an existing search no longer rebuilds results. Eligible source rosters are cached, bestiary probabilities are calculated in batches, and text drawing reuses content to reduce allocations; loot sources, amounts and rates are unchanged.
- Guide crafting queries now support material tracking: select a result to inspect ingredients, owned/required amounts, stations and alternate recipes, then follow materials recursively. Enemy-drop materials fill in bestiary search automatically, with a return to the original path; craftable drops have a separate source button. Shop leaves identify sellers and stage/moon-phase conditions. Queries consume nothing and conversion loops are bounded.
- Terraria Settings → Interface now has Weapon Effects Opacity (0–100%, default 100%), applied immediately and saved automatically. It covers slashes, whips, shots, beams, trails, explosions, glowing particles, summons, Nail Arts and ability effects, including remote shots and summons drawn locally. Damage, hit detection, player visibility and UI are unchanged.
- Shield reflection uses explicit projectile identities instead of guessing from textures, checked against Terraria 1.4.5.8. Imp fireballs and Hornet stingers can reflect; Lightning Strike cannot, despite sharing Thunder Staff artwork. Slow red reflected orbs and directional shield checks are retained. Player thorns are unchanged.

Duplicate single-equipment entries show the probability of receiving at least one, accounting for independent rolls instead of adding their probabilities. Drop rates, quantities and sources are unchanged.

- The Rifle Scope, Sniper Scope and Recon Scope zoom the view out on Hollow Knight's own camera as on the centred Terraria one: with a scope worn, holding the right button pushes the view toward the cursor, half a screen at most; as in 1.4.5, whatever is held, the Sniper Rifle alone pushing two thirds of a screen and 0.8 with a scope.

A Godhome boss's Geo goes straight into your inventory, and an Expert or Master treasure bag keeps the materials bonus until opened.

They hit by the Nail's own damage (its stage, the Nailsmith's upgrades, forging and prefix): the Great Slash and the Dash Slash 2.5 times, once on each enemy; the Cyclone Slash a slash's worth every 0.2 seconds on the enemies in its sweep, ones standing still too. The Nail's slashes and arts are drawn in front, as the Terraria weapons' effects are, and its tooltip lists the arts learned and how to do them.

Building houses in Dirtmouth or any long stay in one room stalls far less and holds much less memory.

- **Buying back from the townsfolk**: what was sold buys back for exactly what it fetched. Worthless things such as weapons and armour made from bars sell for 0 geo and buy back for 0; a forged weapon buys back for its sale price, the forging's share included. As in Terraria, what was bought at a shop since the save was entered sells back for what it cost and goes back on the shelf instead of a buy-back slot.

>   - A boss's arena answers only to a player standing in it: touching its fight trigger from the next room (say, flying to the ceiling of the room under the False Knight) starts no fight and shuts no one out; walking into the arena while still touching the trigger starts it as usual.
>   - Bosses take no damage while they first wake, roar or play their entrance (Terraria weapons, the vanilla nail, damage over time, minions and multiplayer hits alike); roars in the fight proper can be hit as usual.
>   - Minions, sentries, their shots and the Stardust Guardian's punches only take life from bosses and elites: no stagger builds up and the boss never hears the hit, so its attacks are not interrupted and it does not dodge, counter or teleport because of them. The player's own attacks (whips included) work as usual.
>   - Boss loot waits for the death sequence and falls where the body last was (after the maggot bursts out of the False Knight's armour, after the Gruz Mother and the Soul Master burst); bodies that stay (such as the bowing Mantis Lords) wait at most 6 seconds; leave the room before the end and the loot goes straight to your bag. Regular loot, relics and the first-kill Life Crystal are settled together.
>   - Multi-body bosses (Mantis Lords, Soul Master, Watcher Knights, the Nailmaster brothers, Oblobbles, Vengefly Kings) count one kill and drop their loot when the last body falls, bodies not yet out or hidden for now included; Nosk and Galien settle as their death sequence ends.
>   - Boss seals: every boss has a checked landing spot. Seals, creative bestiary challenges and multiplayer followers arrive at the same place, standing on the fight's own floor (the False Knight's upper fight floor, the bottom of Uumuu's fight area, the Soul Master's first-phase floor, the Collector's upper fight area). The Mantis Lords, the Radiance and the seven Warrior's Dreams use the game's own challenge prompts; the Pure Vessel, Oro & Mato, Sheo, Sly, the Oblobbles and the God Tamer use the game's Godhome entrances.
>   - Summoning the Nightmare King Grimm skips the camera pan, the eyes, the silhouette and the title and starts the fight at once. Going straight to Hornet Sentinel at Kingdom's Edge with a seal leaves only her fight body; her story entrance comes back after the challenge.
>   - Rematching a beaten map boss with a seal reloads the game's own fight sub-scene and resets its battle control before the intro and the gates; the Traitor Lord, the Collector and the Crystal Guardian (Guardian's Mirror) can all be fought again.
>   - **Colosseum Ticket**: replaces the Twin Acid Sacs and the God Tamer's whistle; crafted at an anvil from 12 Cobalt Ore + 4 Fireblossom. Used anywhere, it takes you to the door of the Colosseum of Fools, where you sign up for a trial; it is spent on arrival. It cannot be used (and is not spent) in dreams, Godhome, the Colosseum or before stage 4. Old saves' two seals become tickets.
>   - While Hornet pins the Hollow Knight down, the game's own Dream Nail entry stays and Terraria's extra damage, minions and damage over time pause; while the Hollow Knight lies waiting for the Dream Nail before the Radiance, the Terraria Dream Nail, damage over time, minions and sentries cannot hit it.
>   - Using a seal on a bench stands you up first, so sitting is never carried into the fight or a later waking. After losing a Godhome or dream challenge and coming back through an ordinary door to a room with no dream gate (such as from the Oblobbles' challenge back to Kingdom's Edge), the waking is finished: the white screen clears and control, gravity and life come back.

> - **Combat and numbers**
>   - Defense works as in Terraria: each point takes off 0.5 damage on Normal and Hard, 0.75 on Expert and 1 on Master; enemy damage is scaled by difficulty first, then defense comes off, then percentage reductions, set bonuses and shields, at least 1.
>   - Mana Sickness: each mana potion adds 5 seconds (10 at most), and each second left takes 5% off magic damage (50% at most); quick mana and the Mana Flower count, mana stars and natural regeneration do not.
>   - Knockback: an enemy still flying or sliding from a hit is pushed no further by the hits that follow (the Zenith and Star Wrath do not carry an enemy across the room), and those hits still count toward the knockback streak; common enemies and elites knocked back 5 times in 3 seconds stay steady for 1 second. A knocked-back Crawlid keeps to the terrain it crawls on.
>   - Only enemies with a shield on one side (Husk Sentries, Husk Warriors and the like) reflect projectiles; a reflected shot is a slow red energy ball (a quarter of the shot's damage, lasting 6 seconds, gone on hitting terrain). Enemies invulnerable all round (burrowed, hiding in grass, a curled-up Elder Baldur) just absorb shots.
>   - Banners work when carried in the bag, the Piggy Bank or the Banner Box: damage to that enemy ×1.5 on Normal, ×1.75 on Hard and ×2 on Expert and up; damage from it (its shots included) ×0.75 / ×0.625 / ×0.5.
>   - The Stardust Guardian spends its hit cooldown only on a real hit, each guardian on its own, and shields block its punches. Hits blocked by a shield or refused by the game keep the damage remainder saved up so far.
>   - Weapons: Gladius 18, Ruler 15, Chlorophyte Saber 40 (counted as one stab per use); Ash Wood Sword 29, Ash Wood Bow 30, Pearlwood Sword 33, Pearlwood Bow 36 (below their stage's metal weapons); the Gold, Platinum and Tungsten broadswords, shortswords and bows at stage 0; the Gray Zapinator at stage 1, Code 2 and the Revolver at stage 4, the Pulse Bow at stage 5.
>   - The Book of Skulls' skulls last 3 seconds or 640 pixels of flight, hit at most 3 times with a 20-tick cooldown per target, seek within 240 pixels and give up past 480; the Nimbus Rod's and Crimson Rod's clouds last 30 seconds.
>   - The Solar armour's shields circle by the character's facing, and by the direction of use while using an item.
>   - Projectiles made of particles (Water Bolt, the Lunar Flare, sparks and the like) reuse the oldest particles when the pool is full, so they stay visible while casting continuously.
