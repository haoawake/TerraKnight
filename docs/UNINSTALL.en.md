## Uninstalling

Delete `BepInEx/plugins/TerraKnight`, `BepInEx/TerraKnight` and `空洞泰拉.bat` in the Hollow Knight folder, and the "空洞泰拉" desktop shortcut. If you do not want BepInEx either, also delete `BepInEx`, `winhttp.dll`, `doorstop_config.ini`, `.doorstop_version` and `changelog.txt`. The mod's own saves are the `TerraKnight_slot*.json` files in the Hollow Knight save folder.
